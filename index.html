✦ Our Story

Born from a love of sipping slowly

Kynd was created for people who believe that a great drink isn't just about taste — it's about feeling something. We blended the ritual of Japanese tea culture with the warmth of a cozy gathering space.

Every recipe is crafted to nourish you — not just your tastebuds, but your mood. We use ceremonial-grade matcha, ethically sourced ingredients, and a whole lot of heart.
